---
name: Approved Dock interaction language
description: User-approved visual rules for Dock spacing, inactive hover, borders, and active state.
---

Use compact equal spacing around and between desktop Dock items. Inactive items share one thin border color per theme and may receive a flat outline on hover. Active items use a persistent full-tile ring plus a substantial directional edge pill and do not change on hover. Do not use movement, brightness filters, raised shadows, or embossed effects for Dock hover.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved this combined Dock direction on September 14, 2026 after iterating on contrast, hover, active-state clarity, spacing, and border consistency.

**How to apply:** Preserve these principles when changing Dock visuals in either the portfolio or design system. Responsive navigation may retain layout-specific distribution and safe-area spacing, but active indicators must remain visible.
